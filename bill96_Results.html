The cohort strategy built audience infrastructure and nurture engagement but did not independently produce MQL conversions within the campaign window.
Score data caveat: 24 leads show a score of 100+. 22 of these are inflated — by email/bot scanners across buying committee lists, or by Pardot's +100pt automation when an open opportunity exists on an account. The "warm" count is not a reliable metric for this campaign. Only 1 lead outside the MQL list is genuinely warm: Elaine Benoit (Precision Biologic), who downloaded two assets across two campaigns.

Key insights
✓ Marketing cohort — 20,827 enrolled (legacy audience). Avg OR 27.8%, avg CTOR 30.1%, 5,453 total clicks. Strongest absolute click volume by far.
✓ Manufacturing — Most consistent nurture performer. OR grew email over email through E4. Avg CTOR 25.5%.
✓ Tech — Strong overall (avg OR 29.9%, CTOR 32.1%). E2 CTOR collapsed to 6.25% — opened but didn't click — then recovered from E3 onwards.
⚠ Retail — 0% CTR on E3, E4, and E5 despite 20–30% open rates. Opens but no clicks across three consecutive emails — content or CTA not landing for this cohort.
⚠ Consulting — Coldest cohort. Avg OR only 12%. Buying committee avg score was 1.4 — essentially zero prior engagement. E2 and E4 showed 50% CTOR but from very small click bases.
⚠ Manufacturing E5 — OR dropped sharply to 9.6% and 0% CTR after strong E1–E4 performance. Possible fatigue at E5 or content angle didn't land for this cohort at that stage.
